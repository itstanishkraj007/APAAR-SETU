# Product Modules & Roles — APAAR-Setu

APAAR-Setu defines five specialized operational roles:

## 1. Role 1: Institution Registrar (`ROLE_INSTITUTION`)
- **Primary Persona**: College Registrar, Admission Dean, or Nodal Technical Officer.
- **Key Modules**:
  - EoA Sanctioned Seat-Matrix Display (Approved Intake, TFW, FN/OCI, NRI).
  - 3-Way Student Ingestion: Single Entry Form, Bulk CSV Ingestion, Samarth ERP Webhook.
  - Student Roster Table with filterable forensic dossiers.

## 2. Role 2: AICTE Compliance & Vigilance Auditor (`ROLE_AICTE_COMPLIANCE`)
- **Primary Persona**: AICTE Regional Officer (RO), Vigilance Directorate, or Approval Bureau.
- **Key Modules**:
  - National Real-Time Enrolment KPI Grid.
  - Cross-Institution Duplicate APAAR Locks with side-by-side comparison cards and seat cancellation actions.
  - Excess Intake & Unapproved Quota Violations with Show-Cause Notice issuance.
  - AI Anomaly & Ghost-Student Radar with SHAP feature bars and EVC inspection team dispatch.

## 3. Role 3: Policy, Accreditation & AISHE Analyst (`ROLE_ANALYTICS_POLICY`)
- **Primary Persona**: Policy Makers, MoE Statisticians, NBA / NAAC Auditors, NIRF Evaluators.
- **Key Modules**:
  - Regional & Quota Distribution Analytics.
  - AI Policy Copilot with Natural Language query processing.
  - 1-Click Regulatory Exporters (AISHE DCF-II, NBA/NAAC Pack, Foreign Inflow, Excess Violators).

## 4. Role 4: Authorized Stakeholder Verifier (`ROLE_EMPLOYER_VERIFIER`)
- **Primary Persona**: Employers (TCS, Infosys), Foreign Universities (TU Munich), Embassies, Background Check Agencies.
- **Key Modules**:
  - Multi-Identifier Search (APAAR ID or Student Roll ID).
  - QR Code Simulation Scanner.
  - 5-Point Cryptographic Trust Scorecard.
  - Official Dual-ID Verifiable Degree Certificate Studio.

## 5. Role 5: Student Academic Identity Wallet (`ROLE_STUDENT_WALLET`)
- **Primary Persona**: Enrolled Student / Graduate.
- **Key Modules**:
  - National APAAR Identity Card with dual-identifiers and ABC credit balance.
  - DPDP Act 2023 Consent Manager with 24-hour approval and revocation toggles.
